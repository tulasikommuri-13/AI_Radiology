Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upload JPG images?

Yes. The platform supports JPG, JPEG, and PNG chest X-ray images for AI analysis.

Is patient data secure?

Yes. Patient information is stored securely and is only used for generating reports.

How accurate is the AI model?

The AI model provides approximately 98% accuracy on the trained chest X-ray dataset. Final diagnosis should always be confirmed by a medical professional.

Can I download reports?

Yes. After the analysis, you can download a personalized PDF report containing patient details, AI prediction, confidence score, and the uploaded X-ray image.
